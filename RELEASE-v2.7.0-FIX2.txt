Student v2.7.0 FIX2 candidate
Targeted audit: Vocabulary / Stories / Reels

Vocabulary
- Removed client-side switching between local/remote word sources.
- Added server-authoritative student_vocabulary_batch_v271 RPC.
- Exactly 15 words are tied to the user's sequence index and stay unchanged across reopen/login until completion advances the sequence after 08:00 Baghdad.
- Added explicit retry instead of silently switching to a different word corpus.

Stories
- Replaced viewer video playback with Media3 ExoPlayer.
- Added shared bounded media cache and 2 MB next-video warmup.
- Uses actual video duration/progress instead of a fixed fake timer.
- Removed double-next race between timer and player end.
- One active Story video player is reused and released with lifecycle.
- Cached story list renders immediately on reopen while refreshing in background.
- Story uploads stream through a temp file instead of loading the whole video into RAM.

Reels
- Added shared Media3 cache and next-reel prefix prefetch.
- Improved load-control buffering thresholds to reduce repeated rebuffering.
- Cached reel list renders immediately on reopen while refreshing in background.
- Original and 360p reel uploads stream from files instead of readBytes(), reducing OOM risk.
